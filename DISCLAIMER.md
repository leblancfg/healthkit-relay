# Not a medical device

Health Relay copies data from Apple Health to a server you run. That is all it does.

- It is **not a medical device** and has not been reviewed or cleared by the FDA, Health
  Canada, the EU (MDR) or any other regulator.
- It does not diagnose, treat, cure, prevent or monitor any disease or condition, and it
  raises no alerts about your health.
- Data may be incomplete, delayed, duplicated, converted between units or simply wrong, at
  the source (sensors, apps that write to Health) or in transit. Do not rely on it for any
  medical decision.
- Charts and queries you build on this data are yours; they are not medical advice. Talk to
  a qualified health professional about your health.
- In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

The software is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind, as stated in the
[MIT license](LICENSE). You are responsible for securing the server that holds your data and
for complying with the laws that apply to you, especially if you store data about other
people.
